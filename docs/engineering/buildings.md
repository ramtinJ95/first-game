### Buildings

- `BUILDINGS` in `src/sim/buildings/buildings.ts` is the one table: each row's `name`, `gold`, `days`, `needs.people`, `needs.building` when it stands on another, and its own effect fields. `FIRST_LEVELS` lists the five first levels: `forum`, `canals`, `barracks`, `towers`, `temple`. `SECOND_LEVELS` maps each to its second level (`lawCourts`, `reservoirs`, `drillField`, `outerWalls`, `sanctuary`), which needs it, and `GREAT_WORKS` lists `treasuryHall`, `merchantsQuarter`, and `stronghold`, which needs towers. `BUILDING_KINDS` is all thirteen. A city holds `buildings`, the kinds finished, and `construction`, the one at work with its `daysLeft`, or null; `levelOf(city, first)` reads a first level's level, 0, 1 or 2, and `greatWorkOf` the city's work. The city's schema refuses a second level or a work without the building it needs, and two great works counting the one at work. The rules, the AI, and the panel read the table, and every hook reads it through a function beside it: `goldShareOf` and `extraFoodOf` and `levyShareOf` in `dailyBalances` and `limitsOf`, `trainingDaysAt` in `recruit`, `defenceSharesOf` in `fullDefences`, `unrestPaceOf` and `templeReachOf` in `population`, and `tradeShareOf` and `extraSlotsOf` in `endRate` and `routeSlots` in `src/sim/trade/routes.ts`. `endRate(state, route, city)` is the gold a day one end of a route earns, which the trade system pays at arrival, `tradeIncome` counts, and the city's balance lists.
- The second levels and great works came after the thirteenth play-through, when a grown Rome had nothing left to buy; `docs/design.md`'s Buildings line gives their numbers and why. A second level pays back in about five years where it suits, a great work in four to five, and each work is priced under the treasury of the realm it suits.
- The orders `build` and `cancelBuilding` live in `src/sim/buildings/build.ts`. `buildRefusal(state, city, building)` is the one check of whether a city can start a building now, which the order, the AI, and the panel share: built already, work under way, too few people, unrest, siege, the building it needs, a great work held already, then gold. The gold is paid at the order, and a cancel gives back `CANCEL_REFUND` of it. The `construction` system in `src/sim/systems/construction.ts` runs after `economy`, counts each city's work down a day while it is calm and not `beleaguered`, and emits `buildingFinished`.
- `buildingGain(state, city, kind)` in `src/sim/buildings/gains.ts` plays the city with and without a building through `dailyBalances`, `limitsOf`, and `fullDefences`, so a panel never restates a building's number; `gainOf` does the same for several, such as a first level with its second. The building list separately warns when today's stores would waste the extra daily gain, using `dailyBalances` and the finished building's `limitsOf`, or when none of the `buildingGain.calmed` cities is in unrest. These cautions never forbid building.
- A city that falls keeps its finished buildings, levels and work included, for the new owner, and `changeCityOwner` drops the one at work with a `buildingsLost` event. `sack` destroys every building, levels and work included, with a `buildingsLost` event. `enslave` keeps them.
- The AI's rule is `construction` in `src/sim/ai/build.ts`: on a day it raised no recruit, with gold beyond its reserve and the price of the recruit it would raise next (`mostNeeded`), and food counted only while the harvest is tight (`FOOD_MARGIN_MEN`) and balances not falling, it starts the building that pays back soonest by `buildingWorth`, if within `BUILD_PAYBACK_YEARS` for a first level and `PROJECT_PAYBACK_YEARS` for a second level or a great work (`paybackLimit`), and a first level it can start before any second level or work. Outer walls and a Stronghold count as towers do, on a border only, and a Sanctuary as a temple for the nearest foreign city in its longer reach that no temple of the owner reaches. Its constants sit at the top of that file.
- To add a building, add its kind to `BUILDING_KINDS`, its row to `BUILDINGS`, one function beside them that its hook reads, and its numbers to `buildingGain` if a new kind of number. Run `npm run sim:buildings` and put the tables in the pull request.
- The buildings raised `SAVE_FORMAT` to 11, after the 10 of the panels and saves unit.
- The city panel (`cityBuildings` in `src/ui/buildings-text.ts`, drawn by `CityBuildings.svelte`) lists what stands as each first level at its level with both levels' effect and the great work, and offers the first levels, the second levels on those built, and the great works while the city holds none, each with its effect for this city today, its price, days, and payback by `buildingWorth` (`paybackText`).
- `buildingHintOfDay` in `src/app/building-hint.ts` tells once a game, the first day the player's gold stands at `NEAR_LIMIT` (0.9) of its limit while a city of theirs builds nothing and could start a building that pays back within `paybackLimit`, which city, which building, and its payback. A click opens the city and it does not stop the game. The page keeps `buildingWorthGold` in its saved `told`. When another hint, such as the Advances builder's for studying, would fire the same day, this one names the building and the other waits a month.
- The recruit card's Canals and Barracks lines (`canalSite`, `barracksSite` in `src/ui/recruit-forecast.ts`) offer the first level, or in a city that has it the second: "Or build Reservoirs at Roma for 6,500 gold, which grow 5 more food a day once built in 60 days: …" and "Or build a Drill field at Roma for 7,500 gold, which gives 500 more men to call up and 1.0 more a day once built in 60 days: …". The tests hold each named day against a day-by-day replay.
- The second levels and great works added the save change `city-projects`, which carries `olderSavesLoad`: a save from before it holds every building at its first level and loads as it is, as `docs/engineering/independent-changes.md` states.
- `npm run sim:buildings` prints the table, the years each building takes to pay back in a sample of cities with the first choice of each, four Romes at peace for 5 and 10 years: one that does nothing, one that recruits, one that builds, and one that does both by the AI's rules, and the years each second level and great work takes to pay back in Rome's cities on day 1, in year 4 of a peaceful game, and in year 5 of the Rome that builds and recruits, with their targets. `node scripts/sim/projects.ts [--builder panel|ai] [--prices 7500,11000,12000]` plays a peaceful Rome that answers its warnings on 20 seeds for ten years and prints, year by year, its days at the gold limit, its days with nothing at work and nothing worth ordering, and when each great power first holds each price.
- Each great power's own building is a row of `BUILDINGS` with `power`, listed in `SIGNATURE_BUILDINGS` (`colonia`, `cothon`, `royalStables`, `greatGranary`, `royalRoad`). `buildBarrier` refuses it to any other owner, so a conqueror keeps one that stands, with its effect, but cannot start one. Its hooks: `coloniaShareAt` in `unrestDaysFor` (`src/sim/cities.ts`), the Colonia and the stables in `trainingDaysAt(city, troop)`, `harvestShareAt` and `extraStoreDaysOf` in `dailyBalances` and `limitsOf`, the granary's grain in `goldShareOf`, `nearCothon` in `embarkationAt` and `sailKmAt`, and `onRoyalRoad` in `marchKmAt` (`src/sim/march.ts`), which the movement system reads. A Royal road's province is the land nearer its city than any other city, which matches the map's provinces on 94% of the land. The AI weighs them as first levels; `savedManDays` and the Colonia's `conquestWorth` in `src/sim/ai/build.ts` price the ones that save time or unrest, with `CROSSINGS_A_YEAR`, `ROAD_KM` and `CAVALRY_A_YEAR`, and the panel says that basis beside the payback (`PAYBACK_BASES`). The panel offers it under `Your own building` only in a city of its power.
- Each power's standing bonus is a row of `BONUSES` in `src/sim/signatures.ts`, read through `bonusOf(faction, power)` by the rule it changes: `reformDaysOf` in `src/sim/systems/battles.ts`, `guardGoldOf` in `src/sim/guards.ts`, `startingSkillOf` in `src/sim/new-game.ts`, the Nile share in `dailyBalances`, and `adminCitiesOf` with `adminExponentOf` in `src/sim/systems/economy.ts`, whose threshold `src/sim/ai/war.ts` also reads; `evenCities` is where Persia's steeper share meets everyone's. A faction id of a great power is its power id, so a conqueror never gains one. `src/ui/signature-text.ts` says each in one line for the start screen, the Overview, and the Guide. `travelDaysAlong` in `src/sim/march.ts` steps a route at those speeds day by day, so the army panel and the march menu forecast the days the movement takes, and `gatherDaysAt` in `src/sim/orders/embark.ts` gives the wait on a shore to both the order and the menu.
