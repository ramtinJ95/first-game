### World news

- `worldNewsOfDay(before, after, events)` in `src/app/world-news.ts` gives the news of the world that every power hears, in sight or not: a war between two other great powers and their peace, a great capital that falls, a great power destroyed, a new leading power (`leadingPower`, the AI's rule of `LEADER_SHARE`), and a city within `NEAR_KM` of the player's that changes hands between two others, told as ceded when a peace's terms hand it over. It never tells of the player, whose own news `alertsOfDay` gives, and it pauses nothing. An alert's `world` says how far it reaches: `corner` news shows in the corner list, drawn quieter than the player's own, and `chronicle` news, the cities near the player's land, only in the chronicle (`cornerAlerts`). `npm run sim:news` counts the lines a year that the corner and the chronicle get, with Rome idle and under the AI.
- `sightingsOfDay` uses the existing page `told` strings to remember each force's target and last reported strength, so fog flicker and reloading do not repeat an approach. A changed target or growth beyond the last report warrants another alert, without changing the once-per-war pause. `sightingsOfDay` tells fleets of one power seen on one day sailing for one place as one line with their men together, and marching armies the same.
