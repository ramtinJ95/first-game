### World news

- `worldNewsOfDay(before, after, events)` in `src/app/world-news.ts` gives the news of the world that every power hears, in sight or not: a war between two other great powers and their peace, a great capital that falls, a great power destroyed, and a city within `NEAR_KM` of the player's that changes hands between two others, told as ceded when a peace's terms hand it over. It never tells of the player, whose own news `alertsOfDay` gives, and it pauses nothing. An alert's `world` says how far it reaches: `corner` news shows in the corner list, drawn quieter than the player's own, and `chronicle` news, the cities near the player's land, only in the chronicle (`cornerAlerts`). `leaderNews(before, after, told)` tells a new leading power (`leadingPower`, the AI's rule of `LEADER_SHARE`) only when it differs from the leader the player was last told of, and gives the leader told for the next day. A lead that passes through a tie or below the share and comes back to the same power is told once, and every change of the power the others join against is still told. The page starts from the leader of a loaded or new game. On seeds 1 to 5 over ten years, with Rome idle, the old rule told 49 lines for 23 changes of lead, and this one tells 23. `npm run sim:news` counts the lines a year that the corner and the chronicle get, with Rome idle and under the AI.
- `sightingsOfDay` uses the existing page `told` strings to remember each force's target and last reported strength, so fog flicker and reloading do not repeat an approach. A changed target or growth beyond the last report warrants another alert, without changing the once-per-war pause. `sightingsOfDay` tells fleets of one power seen on one day sailing for one place as one line with their men together, and marching armies the same.
