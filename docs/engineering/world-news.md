### World news

- `worldNewsOfDay(before, after, events)` in `src/app/world-news.ts` gives the news of the world that every power hears, in sight or not: a war between two other great powers and their peace, a great capital that falls, a great power destroyed, and a city within `NEAR_KM` of the player's that changes hands between two others, told as ceded when a peace's terms hand it over. It never tells of the player, whose own news `alertsOfDay` gives, and it pauses nothing. An alert's `world` says how far it reaches: `corner` news shows in the corner list, drawn quieter than the player's own, and `chronicle` news, the cities near the player's land, only in the chronicle (`cornerAlerts`). `leaderNews(before, after, told)` tells a new leading power (`leadingPower`, the AI's rule of `LEADER_SHARE`) only when it differs from the leader the player was last told of, and gives the leader told for the next day. A lead that passes through a tie or below the share and comes back to the same power is told once, and every change of the power the others join against is still told. The page starts from the leader of a loaded or new game. On seeds 1 to 5 over ten years, with Rome idle, the old rule told 49 lines for 23 changes of lead, and this one tells 23. `npm run sim:news` counts the lines a year that the corner and the chronicle get, with Rome idle and under the AI.
- `sightingsOfDay` uses the existing page `told` strings to remember each force's target and last reported strength, so fog flicker and reloading do not repeat an approach. A changed target or growth beyond the last report warrants another alert, without changing the once-per-war pause. `sightingsOfDay` tells fleets of one power seen on one day sailing for one place as one line with their men together, and marching armies the same.
- `threatNewsOfDay(before, after, world, told)` in `src/app/threat-news.ts` tells the player of a great power that nears the strength it needs to declare war on it ([Faction AI](faction-ai.md)). It is the player's own news, in the corner, and it stops the game unless the Game menu turns war warnings off. Its `threat:<power>:<day>` keys, the day the power last warned, live in the page's saved `told`, so a reload does not repeat it. A warning that stops the game keeps `threat-stop:<power>:<day>:<share>:<front>`, its day, shown share and the city it weighs or `field`. A later first warning of a notice of that power in the same peace asks no stop when it weighs the same front, stands less than `THREAT_SPARE_RISE` (6) points higher, and its reminder, due 84 days after it, falls within `THREAT_SPARE_DAYS` (200) of the stop. A reminder says war may come any day, so it always stops. No war of a notice comes after its reminder unwarned, so a war on the player always comes within 200 days of a stop for that power. The corner grows only for news that stops the game, so the spared line is told short enough for the corner's 156 px at 1280 and 1024: the share, the front, the day it may declare, and "The game ran on: its stop on Year 5, day 211 was at 85%, and it stands no higher. See At peace in Wars." The At peace row (`standingThreats` with the page's `told`) gives what lowers it, as for every power near war, and adds that it warned again and the stop it ran on from. The chronicle keeps the line. One line names the day's powers, so it stops for all of them or for none, and when it stops each keeps it as its stop. A spared line of several powers gives only their shares and that none stands 6 points above its last stop, and leaves each day of war and stop to its At peace row. A stop the Game menu or `?pauses=off` keeps from stopping the game is not kept, so it spares nothing later. A war or a truce forgets the stop. The rule came from the thirteenth play-through, where Carthage stopped the game four times in 100 days at 80 to 85%; `npm run sim -- threat-repeats` measures it ([evidence](warnings-evidence.md#a-power-that-warns-again-soon-after-it-stopped-the-game)).
- `treasuryAdvice(state, world, power, threat)` in `src/app/threat-news.ts` is the warning's word on a full treasury, in the corner only. When the player's treasury is what takes the power to `NOTICE_SHARE` ("Your treasury of 8,568 gold is what brings it this near: without that gold it would stand at 67%."), or takes `CLEAR_HOARD` (a tenth) or more off the strength it needs ("… takes 25% off the strength it needs."), it names the building the building hint would name (`worthBuilding` in `src/app/building-hint.ts`) and the share the power would stand at once it is ordered, played on a copy of the day through the rules' own handler ("Building Forum at Roma for 6,500 gold would take it to 72%."). It says nothing when no building is worth buying or the purchase leaves the share as it reads; then the plain hoard sentence stands, as it does in the wars panel, which reckons on every refresh and takes no copy of the day.
- `createAnswerNews(world)` in `src/app/answer-news.ts` tells the player what an answer to a war warning bought (`warAnswered`), held against the game played day by day without it. On the first day the player answers a warning that has stood its week, by raising any troop, hiring guards at the city a warning named, or marching an army to within `WAR_FRONT_KM` of it from beyond, it copies the day before and plays the copy on each day after under the AI with the player's orders less every answer. It then tells three things and nothing else: a power that could have declared on the day its warning named and did not, though the copy declared, with its share in each; a power that declared later than the copy did, by how many days; and a power that made peace without the named city, which fell in the copy, with the day. When the copy goes the same way, it says nothing. The copy plays only while a warning may still turn out either way and at most `ANSWER_WATCH_DAYS` (two years) past its first day of war, so a game with no answer pays for none. It is not saved: a load forgets it and claims nothing. The clock hands `onDay` the player's orders it played, which is what the copy plays. `npm run capture -- war-answered` shows the three lines in the corner at 1280 and 1024 wide and in the Chronicle at 390, each from a game on the full map.
