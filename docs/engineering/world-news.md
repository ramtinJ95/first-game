### World news

- `worldNewsOfDay(before, after, events)` in `src/app/world-news.ts` gives the news of the world that every power hears, in sight or not: a war between two other great powers and their peace, a great capital that falls, a great power destroyed, and a city within `NEAR_KM` of the player's that changes hands between two others, told as ceded when a peace's terms hand it over. It never tells of the player, whose own news `alertsOfDay` gives, and it pauses nothing. An alert's `world` says how far it reaches: `corner` news shows in the corner list, drawn quieter than the player's own, and `chronicle` news, the cities near the player's land, only in the chronicle (`cornerAlerts`). `leaderNews(before, after, told)` tells a new leading power (`leadingPower`, the AI's rule of `LEADER_SHARE`) only when it differs from the leader the player was last told of, and gives the leader told for the next day. A lead that passes through a tie or below the share and comes back to the same power is told once, and every change of the power the others join against is still told. The page starts from the leader of a loaded or new game. On seeds 1 to 5 over ten years, with Rome idle, the old rule told 49 lines for 23 changes of lead, and this one tells 23. `npm run sim:news` counts the lines a year that the corner and the chronicle get, with Rome idle and under the AI.
- `sightingsOfDay` uses the existing page `told` strings to remember each force's target and last reported strength, so fog flicker and reloading do not repeat an approach. A changed target or growth beyond the last report warrants another alert, without changing the once-per-war pause. `sightingsOfDay` tells fleets of one power seen on one day sailing for one place as one line with their men together, and marching armies the same.
- `threatNewsOfDay(before, after, world, told)` in `src/app/threat-news.ts` tells the player of a great power that nears the strength it needs to declare war on it ([Faction AI](faction-ai.md)). It is the player's own news, in the corner, and it stops the game unless the Game menu turns war warnings off. Its `threat:<power>:<day>` keys, the day the power last warned, live in the page's saved `told`, so a reload does not repeat it. A warning that stops the game keeps `threat-stop:<power>:<day>:<share>:<front>`, its day, shown share and the city it weighs or `field`. A later first warning of a notice of that power in the same peace asks no stop when it weighs the same front, stands less than `THREAT_SPARE_RISE` (6) points higher, and its reminder, due 84 days after it, falls within `THREAT_SPARE_DAYS` (200) of the stop. A reminder says war may come any day, so it always stops. No war of a notice comes after its reminder unwarned, so a war on the player always comes within 200 days of a stop for that power. The corner grows only for news that stops the game, so the spared line is told short enough for the corner's 156 px at 1280 and 1024: the share, the front, the day it may declare, and "The game ran on: its stop on Year 5, day 211 was at 85%, and it stands no higher. See At peace in Wars." The At peace row (`standingThreats` with the page's `told`) gives what lowers it, as for every power near war, and adds that it warned again and the stop it ran on from. The chronicle keeps the line. One line names the day's powers, so it stops for all of them or for none, and when it stops each keeps it as its stop. A spared line of several powers gives only their shares and that none stands 6 points above its last stop, and leaves each day of war and stop to its At peace row. A stop the Game menu or `?pauses=off` keeps from stopping the game is not kept, so it spares nothing later. A war or a truce forgets the stop. Between its warnings and reminders, a power on notice that stands `THREAT_SPARE_RISE` points or more above the share of its last stop stops the game again ("Carthage has climbed to 89% of the strength it needs near Ariminum, from 82% at its last stop on Year 9, day 225, and may declare any day now."), told in the fewest words of the stops, since the player read the stop it repeats whole, and that stop becomes its last; `threatNewsOfDay`'s `climbs` turns this off for the harness's rule before. Guards that a power on notice counted at the city it weighs, and that stop counting today, `GUARD_DETER_DAYS` after their hire, are told without a stop (`guardsUncounted`), short enough for the corner's smaller room: the share now, when it may declare, and what a new band costs. A power that presses the field armies weighs no guards, so it gets no such line. On the day of a stop the line opens that stop instead ("The 1,000 guards at Roma no longer count."). A warning leaves the day counted guards stop counting, and the share the power would then stand at, to the line `GUARDS_AHEAD_DAYS` before it. A stopping warning of a power whose wars hold back part of its strength (`heldBy` of `warReadiness`) names those wars and the share it would stand at once they end ("Once its war with Carnuntum ends, it would stand at 100%."), reckoned on a copy of the day without them, when that share is higher. The day such a war ends while the power stands on notice, the corner tells it without a stop (`heldWarEnded`): the share now and when the power may declare. On the day of a stop, it opens that stop, as the band line does, and a climb of `THREAT_SPARE_RISE` points the end of the war brings stops the game by the climb rule. The At peace rows leave it out, since a copy of the day for each power would cost the open wars panel too much. The harness's answering Romes do not answer the line, and the sim scripts count only `warThreat` lines as warnings. A stopping warning knows about food. When the troop its advice would have the player raise could not be fed, as the recruit card reckons it (`recruitForecast` in `src/ui/recruit-forecast.ts` runs short of food with the order), a warning by the field armies says "Men raised now would desert: food feeds no more", and names the building the card would (`canalSite`: Canals, or Reservoirs where Canals stand) with its city, price and the men it feeds; and a warning by a city says "Guards at Roma for 90 days lower it and eat nothing; food feeds no more men" in place of the sentence that names more men. Each is said in as few words as the corner allows: the field warning stays within the 355 characters its test holds it to, and `paused-news-fits` reads the longest warning, a climb told with guards that stop counting, in 13 lines or fewer at 1280 and 1024, and a hungry field warning whole. The At peace row of a power at `THREAT_WARN` or more gives the warning's advice too. Only the open wars panel builds the rows: the top bar and the recruit cards count the powers near war with `nearWarPowers`, which builds none, so the forecast costs the open panel about 1.6 ms a rebuild (seed 5, Year 10, one power pressing the field). The fourteenth play-through read the longest warning, 19 lines, whole once and skimmed it after, so the warning leaves to others what they tell: what counts near a city to the At peace notes, and the day guards stop counting to its own line. `GUARDS_AHEAD_DAYS` (10) days before guards that a power on notice counts at the city it weighs stop counting, the corner tells it without a stop (`guardsStopSoon`): the day, and the share the power would then stand at, reckoned on a copy of the day without them. A band hired that day counts from it, so the player can renew the guards before they lapse; on the day they lapse it is too late, since the power decides the morning the player reads the stop. On the day of one power's stop the stop names the day itself, so the line is left out. The answering Romes of the harness follow the advice (`follows`): no answer to a warning that ran the game on, no troop when the line says the men in training will come, and none the food cannot feed, so the guards alone answer; the `-every` Romes answer every line with a troop, as before. The rule came from the thirteenth play-through, where Carthage stopped the game four times in 100 days at 80 to 85%; `npm run sim -- threat-repeats` measures it ([evidence](warnings-evidence.md#a-power-that-warns-again-soon-after-it-stopped-the-game)).
- `treasuryAdvice(state, world, power, threat)` in `src/app/threat-news.ts` is the warning's word on a full treasury, in the corner only. When the player's treasury is what takes the power to `NOTICE_SHARE` ("Without your 8,568 gold it would stand at 67%"), or takes `CLEAR_HOARD` (a tenth) or more off the strength it needs ("Your 8,568 gold takes 25% off the strength it needs"), it names, in the same sentence, the building the building hint would name (`worthBuilding` in `src/app/building-hint.ts`) and the share the power would stand at once it is ordered, played on a copy of the day through the rules' own handler ("; Forum at Roma, 6,500 gold, would take it to 72%."). It says nothing when no building is worth buying or the purchase leaves the share as it reads; then the plain hoard sentence stands, as it does in the wars panel, which reckons on every refresh and takes no copy of the day.
- `createAnswerNews(world)` in `src/app/answer-news.ts` tells the player what an answer to a war warning bought (`warAnswered`), held against the game played day by day without it. On the first day the player answers a warning that has stood its week, by raising any troop, hiring guards at the city a warning named, or marching an army to within `WAR_FRONT_KM` of it from beyond, it copies the day before and plays the copy on each day after under the AI with the player's orders less every answer. It then tells three things and nothing else: a power that could have declared on the day its warning named and did not, though the copy declared, with its share in each; a power that declared later than the copy did, by how many days; and a power that made peace without the named city, which fell in the copy, with the day. When the copy goes the same way, it says nothing. The copy plays only while a warning may still turn out either way and at most `ANSWER_WATCH_DAYS` (two years) past its first day of war, so a game with no answer pays for none. It is not saved: a load forgets it and claims nothing. The clock hands `onDay` the player's orders the rules took, which is what the copy plays, less any order that names an army or a trade route that is not the same record in both games: ids come from one counter, so such a record may carry another id in the copy, and the copy then stops and claims nothing. `onDay` only queues the day; `next` plays it, and the page calls `next` on a frame that played no day of the game, so the copy's day never lengthens a day's frame. A page too slow to give it those frames lets it fall behind; past `ANSWER_BACKLOG_DAYS` (30) days behind, the copy is given up and claims nothing, so it holds no more than 30 days of the game and tells no old news. `node e2e/answer-frame-bench.ts` compares the frames of a game with a copy running against the same days without one. `npm run capture -- war-answered` shows the three lines in the corner at 1280 and 1024 wide and in the Chronicle at 390, each from a game on the full map.
