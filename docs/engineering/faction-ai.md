### Faction AI

- `decideOrders(state, world, faction): Order[]` in `src/sim/ai/decide.ts` gives the orders one faction gives today. It takes a `DeepReadonly<GameState>`, never writes it, and draws no random numbers. Ties go to the lower id, so the same state gives the same orders. `decideAll(state, world, skip = [state.player])` gives the orders of every other faction that is not defeated, in faction id order.
- The clock calls it. `createClock` takes `decide(state)`, and each tick plays the player's queued orders and then `decide(state)` on the day being played. `src/app/main.ts` passes `(state) => decideAll(state, world)`.
- Every threshold is a named constant in `src/sim/ai/thresholds.ts`. Budget: `RESERVE_DAYS`, `MIN_BALANCE_SHARE`, `TROOP_MIX`, `SIGNATURE_SHARE` (each power's own troop takes this share of the place of the shared troop it resembles). War: `WAR_ODDS`, `PEACE_WAR_ODDS`, `SATED_EXPONENT`, `RIVAL_ODDS`, `HOARD_ODDS_SHARE`, `STAY_HOME_PER_YEAR`, `STAY_HOME_CAP`, `RIVAL_PRESSURE_ODDS`, `WAR_DISTANCE_KM`, `WAR_REACH_KM`, `WAR_FRONT_KM`. Offence: `GARRISON_ODDS`, `ATTACK_ODDS`, `DEFENDER_KM`, `SEA_KM_FACTOR`, `STORM_MIN_CHANCE`, `STORM_MAX_LOSS`, `MARCH_MORALE`, `RALLY_WAIT_DAYS`. Defence: `RETREAT_ODDS`, `RELIEF_ODDS`, `RELIEF_KM`, `CAPITAL_GUARD_KM`. Fates: `ENSLAVE_BELOW_MANPOWER`. Minors: `MINOR_DEFICIT_DAYS`. The doc comment on each rule's function in `src/sim/ai` states the rule.
- A great power's day runs in this order: stances, retreats, fates, storms, army movements (capital guard, relief, offence), one recruit or else at most one building (a first level before any second level or great work, [Buildings](buildings.md)) or else at most one advance to study ([Advances](advances.md)), at most one declaration of war. A minor gives orders only at war. Every faction sets the stance of each army in a battle to the one with the best `stanceEdge` over the stances of the enemy's men (`stances` in `src/sim/ai/stance.ts`): freely once in the battle, after that only while the enemy's stances beat its own and no enemy is re-forming, and never while it re-forms itself. It recruits its cheapest troop, keeps its armies at its city once their morale is back to `MARCH_MORALE`, and retreats from a battle it is losing. A minor starts with stores for its cheapest troop and a year of that troop's upkeep (`STARTING_STORES` in `new-game.ts`), so at war it fields a formation that meets the first besieger.
- A great power's standing strength is held by its gold: it recruits only with gold over the reserve for its men (`RESERVE_DAYS` of their pay), and it waits on that on most days, while `MIN_BALANCE_SHARE` rarely binds. Past `ADMIN_CITIES` its income grows only as the fourth root of its cities, so each of its men is paid less too (`PAY_EXPONENT` in `src/sim/systems/economy.ts`, 12 % less at 16 cities and 19 % less at 25). The men a realm's gold keeps, beside a realm of 8 cities, were 1.19 times at 16 cities and 1.33 times at 25, and are now 1.36 and 1.65 times. The AI reads the pay through `goldPerManOf`, in `reserveOf` and `recruitment`, and the player's men are paid by the same rule.
- A great power whose harvest, not its gold, holds its army turns its gold into food (`savingForFood` in `src/sim/ai/study.ts`). Its daily food would not feed its next formation and its daily gold would pay it (`heldByFood` in `src/sim/ai/budget.ts`, by the rules of `recruitment`), and a building or an advance it has yet to start grows food within its payback. On such a day it builds and studies only what grows food, keeps no gold back for the recruit that waits on the food, and opens no trade route that leaves less than its reserve and the price of the food buy that pays back soonest. A power with nothing left that grows food, or one past `ADMIN_CITIES`, spends as before: on top of 153, with no such limit, Persia was the largest power in 11 of `npm run sim -- world`'s 20 seeds, against 8 with it. Building and study read the day after its sacks and enslavements (`afterSpending`), so it never orders a building in a city that the day's fate puts into unrest. The owner chose on 27 November to fix the food, the same way for every power: on seed 8, Rome, held by food, kept the next recruit's price on 448 of the 481 days its gold was ready, spent the rest on towers, forums and trade routes that paid back sooner, and in ten years never studied Fallow fields, which would have fed 1,500 to 3,000 more men. `npm run sim -- food-binds [-- --seeds 1,8 --trace 8:rome]` counts those days for each power and prints the traced power's choices.
- A power weighs a neighbour by that neighbour's armies on land within `WAR_FRONT_KM` of its nearest city plus that city's garrison, with hired guards counted only for `GUARD_DETER_DAYS` (90) from their hire (`weighedGarrison` in `src/sim/guards.ts`), so a rival whose armies are away invites war. It holds back all of an enemy's armies for each war it fights, but not its garrisons, which never leave their walls. Against the leader, a great power with `LEADER_SHARE` of all cities and more than any other, it needs no more than `COALITION_ODDS`. Against a rival great power it needs no more than `RIVAL_ODDS`, and a power past `ADMIN_CITIES` needs less against a rival whose treasury is full: the odds fall by `HOARD_ODDS_SHARE` (0.75) times the share of the rival's treasury that is full times the share of its own cities past `ADMIN_CITIES`, so a power of 16 cities needs three eighths less against a full treasury. Against a rival great power that holds no more cities than the map gave it (`homelandOf` in `war.ts`), the odds it needs, and the rival pressure it weighs, fall by `STAY_HOME_PER_YEAR` (1.5 %) for each year since day 0, to at most `STAY_HOME_CAP` (15 %), as `stayedHome` in `warReadiness` gives it. A rival that holds a city more ends it, and one that loses that city again is weighed so again in full that day: no field remembers it, because a realm back in its first cities has grown no more than one that never left them. Past that number of cities a realm's gold arms no more men than a smaller rival's, and the gold is what a peace would make the rival pay. Against a minor it needs its odds times its cities over the cities the map gave it, raised to `SATED_EXPONENT`, so a realm that has grown is slower to start a new war on a minor. A power at war with no great power also presses a great-power neighbour that has kept to its homeland once its **uncommitted** armies outweigh that neighbour's `RIVAL_PRESSURE_ODDS` times, keeping back the greater of each existing front’s field strength and its city’s `BLOCKADE_ODDS` garrison requirement. This is a planning heuristic in the same strength points as normal garrison scoring: a broken or absent field army does not make an unfinished conquest free. Normal war selection still reserves enemy field armies only. So a player who stays at home is still threatened. Every march the AI orders, the guard of the capital, a relief, and the way back to the muster city included, goes through the router, which prices no march longer than `MAX_MARCH_DAYS` and sails where the land is too far. An army sets out for a city only with `GARRISON_ODDS` times its garrison, counting the armies sent there with it, and only when the men there by the time it arrives, those besieging and those sent there whose road and sea are no longer than its own, hold the blockade. Armies already marching on a target together keep their course only when the first of them to arrive holds the blockade. It leaves for the muster city a siege that cannot hold the blockade, unless armies marching there, and given no other order that day, bring it up to the blockade before the sallies cut down half its men, and those armies keep their march. With no way to the muster city, it steps out of the siege onto land `STEP_OUT_KM` from the city. The muster city is never a besieged one.
- An army that no road and no port of its own takes to its muster city, such as one beaten overseas, sails from the shore it stands on, or marches to the nearest shore first (`wayFromShore` in `src/sim/ai/armies.ts`), and at peace the AI brings home an army that stands on land with no city of its owner. Every crossing the AI orders carries the march on to its target.
- An army of fewer than `REMNANT_MEN` is a remnant: it takes no part in an attack, a relief, or a group, and goes to the muster city, where recruits join it. An idle army marches on an enemy army that stands idle in the field, away from its own cities, within `PREY_KM`, when it outweighs it and every enemy army beside it `ATTACK_ODDS` times.
- Armies become one only by the `joinArmy` order (`src/sim/orders/join-army.ts`): an idle army joins another idle army of its owner within `RALLY_DISTANCE` of it, under the better general, while neither re-forms (`joinRefusal`), and when their stances differ the army re-forms from that day. The player joins with the army panel's Join buttons (`joinChoices` in `src/ui/describe.ts`), so two of the player's armies can stand apart at one city. The AI joins its idle armies at each of its cities that were given no order that day or the same orders, into the lowest id (`withJoins` in `decide.ts`). Each join is weighed against the joins before it, so an army whose join cannot run keeps its orders, and the guard of the capital joins only an army that stays. A recruit joins the idle army at its city, so a power gathers at the muster city and marches and sails as one army.
- The guard of the capital (`guardOfCapital` in `src/sim/ai/armies.ts`) holds an army there only while the enemy armies within `CAPITAL_GUARD_KM` that are not at their own cities could take it: with the men to blockade its garrison, or with any chance to storm it by `stormOdds`. A raid the walls hold alone pins no army. The guard may hunt an enemy army standing within `CAPITAL_GUARD_KM` of the capital, and an army on that hunt stays the guard until it fights.
- The AI caches each collection's sorted order, each faction's enemies, each army's strength, and the day's balances in `WeakMap`s keyed by the record objects. Never change a state's collections after the AI has read it.
- `npm run sim:ai [-- --seeds 20 --years 5]` plays the new game with every faction under the AI, then with Rome giving no orders, and prints the tables the thresholds were tuned on. Both `sim:ai` and `sim:world` print, from `scripts/standoffs.ts`, the armies that stand idle beside an enemy for more than 30 days and the armies idle overseas for as long, with those the menu gives no way home. `scripts/sim-ai-conquest.ts` adds the conquest and economy section by year and the five targets for a world that lasts, each marked met or not.
- `npm run sim:opening [-- --power <id>]` plays the scripted opening of each great power on 5 seeds: war on the nearest minor city on day 1 with the starting army, and the battles, siege, and fall that follow.
- `node scripts/sim-fates.ts` prints what a fallen city adds under each fate, for three sizes over one, two, and five years, and when it is lost on day 90. `node scripts/sim-quiet.ts [-- --seeds 10 --years 5]` counts the days on which the player, Rome, gets no news in the corner list, as the page works the news out, and the day war is first declared on Rome, for a Rome that gives no orders and one under the AI.
- `npm run sim:world [-- --from 1 --seeds 20 --years 10]` plays the new game under the AI and prints, for each seed, the cities of each power, who is largest, who is alive, the sieges begun again after the power ordered its besiegers away, the armies lost to sallies, the longest stretch a power at war sat still with an army that could fight, and the first city each power took. `npm run sim:recruit` has Rome raise every troop it can afford for two years and counts the days it waits on gold and on manpower.
