### The clock and the panels

`src/app/clock.ts` turns real time into ticks and queues orders until the next tick. Panels never read the clock or the live state. They read `view.current` from `src/app/view.svelte.ts`, a `$state.raw` snapshot that is read about ten times a second and published only when one of its fields, or one of its frame's, is a new value. Every panel runs again on a published snapshot, so a field the page builds afresh on each read must be kept until it changes, as `sightFor` and `standingFor` in `main.ts` keep theirs by state. Republished unchanged, the city panel's forecasts took the frames that selecting a city lost (`docs/trade-handover.md`). A click in a panel becomes an order through the clock.
