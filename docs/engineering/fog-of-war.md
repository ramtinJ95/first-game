### Fog of war

- `sightOf(state, world, faction)` in `src/sim/sight.ts` says what a faction sees: the `armies`, `convoys`, `refugeeColumns`, and `battles` in sight, and `sees(point)`. A faction sees the land of its own provinces and within `SIGHT_KM` of its cities, its armies on land, and its convoys. It always sees its own things. A battle with any part in sight is seen with every army in it. The map, the cities, and their owners are always known.
- Sight is only what the player is shown. The rules and the AI never call it.
- A defeated player who watches on sees the whole map: `sightFor` in `src/app/main.ts` gives `sightOfAll` from `src/app/sighted.ts` once the player's `defeatedOn` is set. The top bar then says the realm has fallen in place of the stores, and the defeat's emptying of the stores raises no `storeRanOut` alert.
- `src/app/main.ts` computes the player's sight once per day's state and hands it to the renderer as `sight` and `previousSight`, and to panels as `view.current.sight`. The renderer draws and picks only armies and battles in sight. `seesPick` and `alertsInSight` in `src/app/sighted.ts` keep the selection, a panel's selection, and the alerts to what the player sees, so a selection that leaves sight is cleared and a click on an alert selects nothing hidden. A new panel or alert shows nothing outside `view.current.sight`.
- A wash is an owner's colour laid at `WASH_STRENGTH` (`src/render/palette.ts`) over the ground in the terrain's cached texture: the paper, the desert, and the wood, under the relief, trees, and fields, so every drawing keeps its ink and its lit and shaded sides. A power's wash is light enough for the ground to read through, and a minor's is lighter still. It is laid on with a normal blend, not multiplied, so a colour keeps its hue on the yellow desert, where a multiply turned Carthage's violet into Rome's red and Macedon's blue grey.
- `paintWash` in `src/render/washes.ts` paints the washes on the CPU into a texture of `WASH_CELL_KM` (4 km) cells, which `createWash` shows as one sprite clipped to the provinces' land. A cell is full from `WASH_FADE_KM.full` over land from the nearest cell of another owner and clear at `WASH_FADE_KM.clear`, so a wash runs on unbroken between two provinces of one owner and fades toward the border of another, where the border's ink carries the line. The coast is no border. Over the desert a wash takes up to `WASH_DESERT_GAIN` times its strength, rising over `WASH_DESERT_BLEND_KM` either side of the desert's edge, so a power's hue holds on the sand and the desert's straight outline makes no step in the wash. A change of owner paints the texture again, timed as the user-timing measure `wash`, and calls `terrain.repaint()`.
- The terrain's cached texture draws each border between two provinces once, last, from the edges two outlines share (`sharedBorders` in `src/render/borders.ts`), and the coast line draws the coast. Drawn each frame without a graphics card, the borders took about 75 ms of a 560 ms frame at the widest zoom, and in the cache they change only with the zoom. `borderRuns` cuts each border into runs of a seeded length in km at one of `BORDER_WEIGHTS`, so the ink thickens and thins along the line as a pen's does, the same at every zoom.
- `src/render/fog.ts` draws the `fog` layer. The fog lifts once the player is defeated, who then sees the whole map. Its mask is drawn in km into a texture, so a pan only moves it. The land part is blurred when ownership changes, and each sight circle is a soft disc placed between the two days by the clock's progress.
