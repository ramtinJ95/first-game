### Movement

- An army marches at `armyKmPerDay`, `MARCH_KM_PER_DAY` times the `pace` of its slowest formation, and the panels' days to arrive use it. The AI's own estimates still count every army at `MARCH_KM_PER_DAY`. `ENGAGEMENT_DISTANCE` is a day's march of the fastest troop, so no two armies pass between ticks.
- `src/sim/march.ts` names the speeds `MARCH_KM_PER_DAY`, `CONVOY_KM_PER_DAY`, and `REFUGEE_KM_PER_DAY`. `startMarch` is the one way to set an army marching: it finds the land path, or returns why there is none. No march takes longer than `MAX_MARCH_DAYS` (90), so a march round a sea is refused and the way by sea is the answer. `marchPath` gives the path with that check.
- A marching army, a convoy, and a refugee column hold the `waypoints` still ahead. The path is found once, when the march starts, and `src/sim/systems/movement.ts` follows it. Movement runs first in the day after `homecoming`.
- A retreating army walks its path like a marching one, but takes no order and fights no battle until it arrives and stands idle.
- `moveArmy` replaces any march, `halt` stops one, and `embark` puts an army aboard a convoy to another port. `embarkationAt` in `orders/embark.ts` says where it sails from: within `AT_PORT_KM` of its faction's port city or that city's harbour the ships are ready, and on any other shore within `SHORE_KM` of the sea, an enemy or neutral port included, the convoy waits `GATHER_DAYS` (`convoy.departsOn`) for its ships, so no army overseas is shut in. A besieging army may embark and leaves its siege. The order's `then` is where the army marches on once it lands. The land path is found when it embarks and kept on the embarked activity as `march`. Without `then`, an army bound for a port whose harbour lies farther than `SIEGE_DISTANCE` from the city marches into the city, so it stands at it and besieges an enemy's. Otherwise the convoy lands its armies idle at the destination harbour. With `from`, the army first marches to that shore or port, and the order is refused unless the crossing could set out from there today. The march carries the crossing as `sail`, and keeps it through a battle it wins. `movement` leaves an arrived march with a `sail` standing on its last point, and `systems/embarkation.ts`, which runs next, sets it out there. Ships gather from the next day, so the menu's days to the shore, to gather ships, and by sea add up to the days played. When it can no longer sail from there, as when the port it marched to has changed hands, it stands idle and a `sailingRefused` event tells the player why. The menu sends one such order for a way by sea that starts with a march, so no order leaves an army at the shore. `evacuate` gives a new refugee column its land path, and the column ends its walk at its destination with no waypoints, where `population` settles it.
- `npm run sim:paths` reruns the path-finding experiment, `npm run sim:movement` prints the route table and the tick times, and `npm run map:routes` draws routes on the map preview into `captures/movement/`.
