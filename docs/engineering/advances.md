### Advances

- `ADVANCES` in `src/sim/advances/advances.ts` is the one table: each row's `name`, `branch` (`military`, `economy`, `statecraft`), `gold`, and `days`, and its own effect field. `ADVANCE_KINDS` lists them in the order the panel shows them and a tie goes. A faction holds `advances`, the kinds it finished, and `study`, the one at work with its `daysLeft`, or null. Every hook reads the table through one function beside it, which takes the faction record: `generalSkillOf` where an army is mustered and when Drillmasters finish (`study` system), `rationShareOf` in `foodPerManOf` (economy and the recruit forecast), `wallWearShareOf` in `wallWear` (`sieges.ts`, `city-danger.ts`), `coinShareOf`, `harvestShareOf` and `rollsShareOf` in `dailyBalances` and `limitsOf`, `marketShareOf` in `endShareOf` (`trade/routes.ts`, the AI's trade), `unrestShareOf` in `unrestDaysFor` (`changeCityOwner`, `sack`, `enslave`), and `guardShareOf` in `guardDaysFor` (`hireGuards`). A band hired for longer than `GUARD_DAYS` names its `hiredOn`, so great powers still count it only `GUARD_DETER_DAYS`, a full city may replace it from then (`replaced` takes the band hired first, by `hiredOn(band)`), and the guards news and panel date it by its hire and its own last day.
- A realm studies one advance at a time, in any order, each once. The orders `study` and `cancelStudy` live in `src/sim/advances/study.ts`. `studyRefusal(state, faction, advance)` is the one check of whether a realm can start an advance now, which the order, the AI, and the panel share. The price is paid at the order and kept in the study as `paid`, and giving up gives back `STUDY_REFUND` of what was paid. `studyPrice(state, faction, advance)` is the price: the listed `gold`, or for the `GROWING_ADVANCES` (Standard coin, Fallow fields, Market weights, Citizen rolls), whose gain grows with every city, the listed gold times the realm's cities over `PRICED_CITIES` (8) once it holds more. The order, the refusal, the AI's payback and the panel all read it, and the panel says why a price is above the listed one (`priceWhy`). The `study` system in `src/sim/systems/study.ts` runs after `construction`, counts each faction's study down a day, and emits `advanceFinished`. Study goes on through wars, sieges, and unrest; it stops only with the realm.
- `advanceGain(state, faction, kind)` in `src/sim/advances/gains.ts` plays the realm with and without the advance through `dailyBalances` and `limitsOf`, so a panel never restates an advance's number. An advance whose effect those sums do not show adds its own number there, as `buildingGain` does for walls.
- The AI's rule is `studies` in `src/sim/ai/study.ts`: on a day a great power raised no recruit and started no building, and studies nothing, with gold beyond its reserve (`reserveOf(state, faction, 'gold')`, so a sum another rule keeps in that reserve is never spent on study) and the price of the recruit it would raise next, it starts the advance that pays back soonest by `advanceWorth`, if within `STUDY_PAYBACK_YEARS` and no later than the building it would start next with gold enough (`nextBuilding` in `build.ts`, which `construction` also uses). An advance bought on a day the next building was out of reach put that building off, and a Rome the AI played lost 28 cities in the slide's 20 games against 12 on `main`, so an advance never waits ahead of a building that pays sooner. The AI's building is as before. `advanceWorth` prices gold, food and manpower as `buildingWorth` does, Drillmasters as the upkeep of the men their harder blows stand for (`sharperMen`), Siege engineers as the besiegers' upkeep over the days they save and Clemency as the output it saves, each in `CONQUESTS_A_YEAR` conquests of a city like the nearest foreign one (`nearestTarget`), and Long contracts at nothing: a band counts `GUARD_DETER_DAYS` against a threat whatever its term, and a band kept to defend a city cannot be told from one hired to deter, so the AI never studies it and the panel gives it no payback in years but says where it saves gold (owner's decision of 25 November, after a Rome that hired guards only to answer warnings bought it in seed 28 and put its buildings off). `bestStudy` is the same choice for any budget and payback limit. The panel marks the one it gives the player for all the gold in the treasury, unless a city of the player's could start a building that pays back sooner by the building hint's reckoning (`worthBuilding`); then it marks no advance and names that building, its city and its payback in a line above the branches, with the gold it lacks and the days today's income takes to bring it (`marked` and `buildingFirstLine` in `src/ui/advances-text.ts`). A building the treasury cannot pay for yet has its payback counted from today, those days plus its payback; an advance the treasury pays for now that pays back sooner than that is marked after all, and with no day to promise (a price above the treasury's limit, or no income) any such advance is. The line then gives both sides and how far studying puts the building off, and the player chooses. Only the panel reads this; the AI's rule is the owner's of 25 November. A Temple or a Sanctuary, whose payback is reckoned from a city taken each year, is weighed there only by a realm that holds a city the map gave another (`hasConquered`). The advance it marks is also named at the panel's top, with a button that brings its card into view (`markedLine`). A studied advance still says what it gives the realm now; Drillmasters counts its armies at skill 5 against generals of the starting skill (`drilledMen`).
- The page shows the realm's advances in `src/ui/AdvancesPanel.svelte`, the `advances` panel of `panel` in `main.ts`, from `advancesView` in `src/ui/advances-text.ts`: the three branches, and for each advance what it does (`ADVANCE_EFFECTS`) and what it would do for the realm today (`doesText`, from `advanceGain` and the worth's own reckonings), its price, its days, its payback, and Study or the reason it cannot start. The top bar's Advances button names what is studied and its days left, or says that nothing is (`studyLine`), and opens the panel.
- `advanceNewsOfDay` in `src/app/advance-news.ts` tells the player of each advance its realm finished, in the corner and the Chronicle. When nothing else is being studied and an advance is still open, the line says to choose the next and stops the game (pause kind `advance`, "An advance is finished and nothing else is being studied" in the Game menu, on by default, off with `pauses=off`): the owner's answer of 25 November, "Stop, with a switch". It stops only for the player's realm, at most once for each of the nine. The measuring Romes in `scripts/` play in Node through `tick` and never meet the page's stops. Once a game, on the first day the treasury stands at `HINT_SHARE` of its limit while the realm has studied nothing and studies nothing, it gives the hint that the Advances panel exists. The page's saved `told` keeps `advance-hint`. A click on either opens the panel (`Alert.opens`).
- A save from before advances loads with nothing studied: the change file `src/sim/save-changes/advances.json` sets `olderSavesLoad`, and the faction's schema defaults both fields.
- To add an advance, add its kind to `ADVANCE_KINDS`, its row to `ADVANCES`, and one function beside them that the rule it changes reads. Its number reaches `advanceGain` through `dailyBalances` and `limitsOf`, or add it there if it is another kind of number, and `advanceWorth` prices it. Run `npm run sim -- advances` and put its tables in the pull request.
- `npm run sim -- advances [-- --players ruler,answering,stranger --seeds 20 --years 10]` plays the slide's Romes at peace and prints, a year, the days Rome's gold stands at its limit, the gold the limit throws away, and the gold Rome could have spent without giving anything up. `stranger` plays as `answering` does but never builds, the player of the thirteenth play-through whose gold sat at its limit.
